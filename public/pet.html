Boarding

  • Cat Boarding

    The cattery room has views of the outdoors and a
    big screen TV. Individually vented condos have separate
    rooms for the litter box with high shelves to lounge on.
    There are no wire cages, no fencing or wire mesh.

    In preperation for boarding, you cat needs to have
    current vaccinations. Please provide proof of current
    status during the first check in and annually thereafter.
    We recommend the pet food which your pet is already
    accustomed to eating.

  • Dog Boarding

    Our canine guests enjoy the view, shaded by huge white
    pines, oaks, and maples, complete with wildlife such as
    rabbits, squirrels and birds. Our buildings are gas heated
    and climate controlled for even temperatures. Runs are
    made of natural crushed limestone or sealed concrete.

    In preperation for boarding, you dog needs to have
    current vaccinations. Please provide proof of current
    status during the first check in and annually thereafter.
    We recommend the pet food which your pet is already
    accustomed to eating.
